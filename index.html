Our story

Built for the good kind of slow morning

Madden’s started as a simple notion: proper coffee, made with care, served in a room you actually want to linger in. We pour for flavour over fuss — flat whites with a tidy heart on top, beans we’re proud of, and a counter where your name gets remembered fast.

We’re a Tralee café through and through — a bit of vintage charm, mismatched chairs, good chat and better cake. The whole place runs on one quiet motto printed on every cup: there’s no place like home. So we built one you can walk straight into.
